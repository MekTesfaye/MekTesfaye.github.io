About

Hi, I'm Mekdim (most people call me Mek).

I do marketing and communications for industries that are hard to explain. For five years that has meant aerospace and geospatial software, at Aerospace New Zealand, Esri Australia and ChristchurchNZ: campaigns, events, PR and content.

I trained as an aircraft engineer at Air New Zealand before moving into marketing. So when an engineer explains what they've built, I follow it. Then I work out why a customer, a sponsor or the public should care.

I'm based on the Gold Coast, with full Australian working rights as an NZ citizen. I'm looking for marketing and communications roles in aerospace, technology and innovation, and I take on freelance projects too.
