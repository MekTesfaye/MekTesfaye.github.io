About

Hi, I'm Mekdim (most people call me Mek).

I've spent five years in marketing and communications for Esri Australia, Aerospace New Zealand and ChristchurchNZ. That covers campaigns, virtual and in-person events, PR, content and design, usually from first brief to the last attendee leaving.

Before marketing, I trained as an aircraft engineer at Air New Zealand. I still use CAD, product design and rapid prototyping via additive manufacturing, and I co-founded a startup that flew a camera above 100,000 ft. That background is my unfair advantage. I can sit with engineers, understand what they've built, and turn it into something customers, sponsors and journalists actually care about.

I'm based on the Gold Coast, I'm an NZ citizen with full Australian working rights, and I'm looking for marketing and communications roles in aerospace, technology and innovation.
